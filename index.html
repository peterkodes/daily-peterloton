Editor's note

Sports and sport followers need heroes or heroic stories. The people doing extraordinary stuff on tracks, fields, bikes, pools, they are as big as the distinction they are able to achieve. It's not different in cycling, where Demi Vollering wins everything, is at the top of the world, does amazing things, and yet she never has the same support as Marianne Vos or even Annemiek van Vleuten. There are other examples, much like her, in the women's peloton: Anna van der Breggen, Lotte Kopecky. They should all be all the rage, but somehow they fade as soon as somebody takes away their UCI jerseys. And the men's is no different. Is Pogačar the GOAT? There was a lot more excitement when Sagan was in contention. And even Van der Poel and Van Aert raise the temperature more than Pogačar vs Evenepoel or Vingegaard. What do you think? Have I got it wrong? Are Pogačar and Vollering GOAT candidates, and if so, do they fill the superhero aspect?

Peter, editor · N5 · Newsroom

RoadN5 · Newsroom

Roglič wins a time trial. The "fairytale" is doing a lot of work.

Cyclingnews wants you to believe in a fairytale. I don't. Primož Roglič, 36, took the European time trial title in Šenčur on 7 October, and the headline cheerfully adds "no Pogačar, no problem." Spot the flaw: Pogačar was absent after his Vuelta crash, so what we watched was a vacancy being filled, not a rivalry being settled. Slovenia had gone thirteen events without a medal, which says more about the depth of their programme than about anyone's genius. Even the dead radio in the first kilometre is spun as heroic intuition, when a rider without a radio simply rides blind into a headwind. Pleasant result. Not a statement.

Mountain bikeN5 · Newsroom

Van Rysel's first MTB rolls on 32-inch wheels

Okay so like, Van Rysel just dropped its very first mountain bike, the RCR-XC, and it's rolling on 32-inch wheels. Thirty-two! Cross-country racing has basically lived on 29ers forever, so this is kind of a big deal. Van Rysel says the carbon frame is 230 grams lighter than the Decathlon Ford team's current bike, and its wind tunnel numbers supposedly show a 15.5-watt saving at 35 km/h, which is, like, actually wild if it holds up. The team starts racing it in 2027, with Zipp, RockShox and Vittoria in on the development. But real talk? No price, no suspension travel, no release date yet. So it's super hyped, but we can't tell if it's, you know, a bike we can actually buy. Stay tuned!

GravelN5 · Newsroom

Colnago G5-X: 53 mm clearance, 27.2 mm post, frame storage

Colnago's G5-X is the third generation of the brand's gravel frame, and the changes are mechanical and specific. The seatpost moves to a slim, round 27.2 mm diameter, which allows a dropper. Tyre clearance is 53 mm, with double-chainring compatibility retained. Geometry gets a slacker head angle, longer wheelbase, lower bottom bracket and increased fork rake. Claimed weights are 950 g for a medium frame and 420 g for the fork. Storage is added at the top tube and down tube, using magnetic Fidlock cages, along with extra bottle and fork mounts. The revised carbon layup targets compliance without reducing drivetrain stiffness. Prices run from £5,299 to £9,999 for the Red AXS XPLR. US pricing and availability are unconfirmed.

Women's cyclingN5 · Newsroom

Reusser wins a fifth title; Vollering answers with silver

Marlen Reusser took her fifth European time trial title in Šenčur, beating Demi Vollering by 23 seconds, and the details are why women's racing is so hard to look away from. Reusser was fastest at every checkpoint on a 22 km course where two climbs, Možjanca and Štefanja Gora, settled everything. Vollering, who had won the road race solo only days earlier, still came back for silver. France's Juliette Berthet took bronze, six seconds clear of Elisa Longo Borghini, who had missed a road-race medal when a crash in the final corner spoiled her sprint. Nobody here needed a missing star to make the day dramatic.

AliExpress kitN5 · Newsroom

AliExpress glasses: 4.9 stars, but read the small print

This week's kit check is cycling glasses on AliExpress. The most trusted pair I can point to is the ROCKBROS UV400 model with a myopia-compatible frame: 4.9 stars, more than 10,000 sold, $18.74 as listed. For photochromic lenses, the Kapvoe HD pair also scores 4.9 at $17.70, but with only 284 sales, so that rating rests on far fewer buyers. Then there are the $0.33 photochromic pairs with tens of thousands of sales and the same 4.9; at that price I'd treat the stars as noise, not proof. A caveat on all of them: these are the shops' own listings, lens details appear only in the titles, and nothing I read confirms real optical quality.
